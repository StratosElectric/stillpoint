Stillpoint app privacy policy

Stratos Electric · Effective October 31, 2026

The Stillpoint app (Android and iPhone) works with the Stillpoint Puck to balance ceiling fans. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: the app does not collect, send or sell any personal information. Everything it stores stays on your phone.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Which Puck your phone is paired with, and your settings (weight sizes, wobble limits, motor type).
  • The last fan you set up (its size, make, model, how it hangs and how it turns on), so the app can ask "Same fan as last time?".
  • The job in progress, so it can pick up where it left off if the app is closed.
  • The jobs you save: job number, date, how long the job took, the room name and notes you type, the fan's size, make, model, how it hangs and how it turns on, the weights used, the wobble before and after, and for a Quick Check whether you could see the wobble.

This information is kept only in the app's storage on your phone. Stratos Electric never receives it. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If your phone is backed up (to iCloud or your Google account), the backup can include the app's data; Apple or Google keep it under your agreement with them.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to find and talk to your Stillpoint Puck: to take measurements, calibrate it and update its software. It does not look for or connect to other devices.

Location (older Android phones only)

Android 11 and older only let apps find Bluetooth devices when Location permission is granted and Location is turned on. On those phones the app asks for it for that reason only. The app never reads, stores or sends your location. Newer Android phones and iPhones do not ask for location.

Sharing a job report

When you tap Share Report, the app opens your phone's share menu with a text report of the job. It goes only where you choose to send it (a text message, email, and so on). The app sends nothing on its own.

No accounts, no tracking, no ads

The app has no sign-in and no ads. It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising software, and it does not connect to the internet. It does not share information with third parties.

Children

The app is a tool for balancing ceiling fans and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
