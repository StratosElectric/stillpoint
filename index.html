Stillpoint app support

Stratos Electric

Stillpoint balances ceiling fans with the Stillpoint Puck and your phone (Android or iPhone).

Questions or problems? Email support@stratoselectric.com. Tell us your phone model and what the app's screen says.

The app can't find my Puck

  1. The Puck is on: press the big button on the front. Its screen says HELLO, not a status light.
  2. The phone is within a few feet of the Puck, with Bluetooth on.
  3. Turn the Puck off and on: hold the big button 3 seconds, then press it.
  4. Stillpoint is allowed to use Bluetooth. Android: Settings, Apps, Stillpoint, Permissions, Nearby devices. iPhone: Settings, Stillpoint, Bluetooth.
  5. Android 11 and older: Location must be on while searching (Android's rule for Bluetooth). Stillpoint does not use your location.
  6. Another phone with Stillpoint open nearby may be connected to the Puck: close the app there.

Trying the app without a Puck

Tap Try the demo on the first screen, or Demo on Home. The demo runs a whole balance on a simulated Puck and fan. Nothing you do in the demo changes your real jobs or settings.

Balancing a fan

  • The Puck goes on top of the fan's motor housing, with the fan off.
  • A balance takes about 10 minutes and 5 runs. The app tells you when to turn the fan on and off, where to put the test weight, and which weight goes on which blade.
  • Quick Check runs once and tells you if the fan needs balancing at all.
  • The Puck calibrates itself when it turns on. To calibrate it again: Settings, Calibrate Puck, with the Puck on the fan (fan off and still) or on a table. It takes 3 seconds.

Updating the Puck

When the app has newer software for your Puck, Home says Puck update ready. Go to Settings, Puck software, Update. Keep the Puck on and the phone close to it; it takes a few minutes. You can switch to another app, but don't close Stillpoint. If an update stops partway, the Puck keeps its old software: just tap Try again.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your jobs stay on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
